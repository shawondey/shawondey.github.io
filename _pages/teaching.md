My teaching interests include control systems, robotics and autonomous systems, nonlinear systems, and related areas in electrical and computer engineering. I aim to connect theoretical concepts with practical engineering applications, particularly in robotics, aerial systems, and autonomous systems.

## New Mexico State University

### EE 492 — Introduction to Control Systems
**Instructor, Fall 2026**

Klipsch School of Electrical and Computer Engineering  
New Mexico State University

This course introduces fundamental concepts in classical control systems, including mathematical modeling, transfer functions, system response, stability, root locus, frequency response, and feedback controller design. MATLAB and Simulink are used to connect theoretical concepts with practical engineering applications.

## Teaching Philosophy

My teaching approach emphasizes developing strong fundamentals while connecting mathematical concepts to physical systems and engineering applications. In control-related courses, I use examples from robotics, UAVs, autonomous systems, and other dynamical systems to help students build both analytical understanding and practical intuition.

I also emphasize computational tools such as MATLAB and Simulink to complement theoretical analysis and help students understand how control-system concepts are applied in practice.
