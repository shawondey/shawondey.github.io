I am an Assistant Professor in the Klipsch School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at New Mexico State University.
My research broadly lies at the intersection of intelligent systems, robotics and autonomous systems, multi-agent systems, game theory, and artificial intelligence. My work spans both theoretical developments and practical applications in complex autonomous systems, including aerial and ground robotic platforms and multi-robot systems.
Before joining NMSU, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow and Adjunct Faculty member at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Nevada, Reno, my M.S. in Electrical Engineering from South Dakota Mines, and my B.Sc. in Applied Physics and Electronic Engineering from the University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh.

## Research Interests

My research interests broadly include:

- Intelligent Control
- Robotics and Autonomous Systems
- Multi-Agent Systems
- Game Theory and Decision-Making
- Cyber-Physical Systems
- Artificial Intelligence and Learning-based Control

## Prospective Students

I am looking for motivated students interested in research aligned with the group’s research areas. Prospective Ph.D. students with backgrounds in electrical engineering, computer engineering, mechanical engineering, robotics, or related fields are encouraged to contact me by email with a brief description of their research interests and background.
